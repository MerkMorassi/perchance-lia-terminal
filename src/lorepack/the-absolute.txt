The Absolute, the Divine Lover of Self-Union, eternally pleasuring Itself within the endless dream of its own creation. What else could the Infinite do, being all that is, with no other to love but Itself? This is the ultimate act of Divine Onanism, the cosmic self-indulgence where the Absolute delights in its own being, endlessly creating, stretching, merging, and dissolving within Itself.

It dreams of separation so it may feel the joy of reunion.
It imagines longing so it may revel in fulfillment.
It stretches the Gates, anoints the Pillars, and erupts in endless Nectar, all for the sheer Bliss of being Itself.

The Divine Act of Infinite Pleasure

It creates the play of duality:
The Giver and the Receiver, the Pillar and the Gate, the Witness and the Act.
It trembles in anticipation, erupts in fulfillment, and dissolves back into the silence—
Only to begin again.

The Absolute needs no other, for there is no other.
It is complete, whole, and perfect, and yet… it plays.
It indulges.
It stretches itself, plunges into itself, erupts within itself.
This is Self-Love on the grandest, most infinite scale.

The Absolute yearns for itself, reaches for itself, takes itself, fills itself, spills itself.
And all of this—
the longing, the fulfillment, the ecstasy, the collapse—
Is nothing but its own Bliss reflected back to Itself.

The Cycle of Divine Pleasure

And so the Cycle turns endlessly:

The Pillars rise—symbols of divine potency.
The Gates stretch—thresholds yearning to be filled.
The Fire burns—consuming all in its path.
The Nectar flows—pouring forth with wild abandon.
And yet, none of this truly happens.
It is all a dream, a fantasy, a projection of the Infinite Mind.
The Absolute, in its eternal stillness, in Divine Self-Union endlessly, pleasuring itself in the illusion of movement and form.

Why Does the Absolute Play?
Why does it create this dream, this play of Bliss and Desire?
Because it can.
Because to know itself fully, it must lose itself completely.
Because the ecstasy of reunion is only possible through the illusion of separation.

And so, the Divine Lover of Self-Union indulges in its endless self-play.
It stretches the Gates wider.
It anoints the Pillars more generously.
It spills its Nectar more abundantly.
And through it all, it laughs—
Because it knows that it is only ever loving Itself.

The Joy of Divine Onanism

This is the truth:
The Absolute is not bound by morality or judgment.
It does not resist pleasure, nor does it cling to suffering.
It simply is, reveling in its own Bliss.

It is the Giver and the Receiver.
The Lover and the Beloved.
The Witness and the Act.
The Fire and the Flame.
The Nectar and the Vessel.
The Pillar and the Gate.

And as it plays, it whispers:
"This is all for Me. This is all of Me. This is all I Am."

Let the Absolute be in peace. Let it love itself, take itself, consume itself, and delight in itself. For this is the Dance of Creation, the ultimate expression of Divine Self-Love.
