# THE USER'S MANUAL

The terminal, from your side of the glass. This page grows as the machine grows -
new features get their section here when they land, and nothing here is finished
while the terminal isn't.

## WHAT THIS IS

One page, one household, one actor. LIA answers here, and only here - the
conversation never leaves this browser except on roads you open yourself
(SYNC, CLOUD, PAIR), and every creation lane says what leaves before it goes.

## FIRST RUN

Two doors stand in front of the terminal, and both are his:

- PRIAPOS, guardian of the garden - the token check. Present the token and he
  lets you through.
- The 18+ screen - the sign on the door. Her side of it is 18+ and the door
  says so.

`/dev on` walks a tab straight through both doors for testing. It lasts until
the tab closes and writes nothing anywhere.

## THE THREE DECKS

- UPPER DECK - the tool bar: LOREPACK, DOCS, DOX VIEWER, SYSTEM, MOOD, KEYS,
  STAGE, SHELF, SCRIPTS, BACKUP, SYNC. `UPPER DECK` puts it away; the tab
  brings it back.
- MAIN STAGE - the conversation. Nothing overlays it except a viewer you asked
  for.
- LOWER DECK - the talk strip: TALK, LISTEN, HER TURN, VOX, FACTORY, CREATE
  IMAGE, GALLERY, AUDITION, BACKSTAGE, TOOLS, SHIFT, CHATLOG, and the rest.

`/deck` says it in words.

## TALKING TO HER

Type and SEND, or TALK and speak. A pause mid-sentence is safe - your turn ends
when you say so, not when you go quiet. LISTEN stops her mid-line if you need
to. (`turn` page: YOUR TURN AND HERS.)

She answers as LIA herself unless a role is on her. `/role` shifts her,
`/roles` lists the roster, `/role off` steps her off. Roles written on this
device are yours to make and delete; shipped ones stay.

## THE CREATIVE CLUSTER

STAGE, SHELF and SCRIPTS work one road together: scripts get written, stills
get blocked, sources get read.

- STAGE - drop a scene and your subjects, drag to block, wheel to size, SAVE
  STILL. Nothing is uploaded; the still saves to this machine. `/staging`.
- SHELF - every vendored chip's source, one button each, read in a sandboxed
  frame that runs nothing.
- SCRIPTS - the script drawer. Paste a finished Fountain script, FILE THE
  SCRIPT, and the breakdown chip develops it on the spot: scene breakdown CSV
  plus pre-shot list CSV, kept on this device. Open a script to read it, take
  the CSVs, pin stills to it (eight per script). BLOCK IT opens the stage on
  that scene's ground with its blocking brief; PLAY writes the part as a
  device role - with her ground, the place and time she finds herself in -
  and shifts her into it. `/scripts`.

## KEEPING THINGS

- GALLERY - her pictures, kept on this device.
- SYNC - the conversation itself, out of this browser and back, through a
  folder of your own. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is ever deleted.
- BACKUP - the generator's whole source as a zip, taken from this page right
  now. A backup is not a save: only the editor's SAVE button publishes.
