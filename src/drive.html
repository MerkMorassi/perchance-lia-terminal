A FOLDER ON YOUR OWN DISK

This little window is the one place the browser lets a folder be opened, so the drive lives here and the terminal talks to it. Pick a folder once - a mapped drive, an external disk, a photo library - and the terminal can read every picture in it and write its own back.

Reading and writing both happen in this window because the terminal is embedded, and browsers refuse a folder picker inside an embedded page. Chrome and Edge have this API; Firefox and Safari do not - there the vault still works on its own storage. Pulling again only brings in what is new, and the pictures are written into a Mythos subfolder - never over the top of the folder's own files, and never pulled back in on a later scan. NOTHING HERE IS UPLOADED ANYWHERE: the folder is yours, on your disk.
