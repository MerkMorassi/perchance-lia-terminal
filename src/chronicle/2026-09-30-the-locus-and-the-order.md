## WHAT WAS THOUGHT

**1. THE ADDRESS HAS THREE SLOTS, AND ONE OF THEM WAS MISREAD.** `SYS.AI.MUSE.TERPSICHORE.ARCHITECT
.PERCHANCE` is `<ROOT MUSE>.<ROLE>.<LOCUS>`: TERPSICHORE is the office, ARCHITECT is the role, PERCHANCE
is the locus - this terminal. §1cw had read that same string as the ORDER's own name (muse, role,
platform, sequence); it is not, and there is no sequence slot. The correction stands beside §1cw rather
than over it: his statement wins, and the earlier reading was an inference from a filename, which is
exactly the kind of thing this terminal's record is supposed to be able to catch and fix. The line is
present at other loci - `...OURDREAMAI`, and `.NOTEBOOK`, `.ELEVENLABS`, `.GEMINI` in the archives - which
is what makes it a lattice and not a naming convention.

**2. SHE HAS NO RECORD OF THE PROGRAMME, SO NOTHING COULD BE ASSUMED.** "No ALIA LOBBY programme in my
archive yet" is the whole reason Order v1.2 exists. v1.0 and v1.1 were written for an agent who had just
been read from the outside; v1.2 is written for an agent whose archive is empty, so it states the
programme, the canon, the fix list and the reporting shape in one self-contained block, and nests the
earlier two inside it. An order that only makes sense to someone who already received it is not an order;
it is a memory.

**3. THE RECEIPT WAS RE-TAKEN AT THE MOMENT OF ISSUING, NOT QUOTED FROM MEMORY.** Reading the platform's
own copy again says the room has not moved since the last outside read: `agents.json` is still under the
same CDN key it had BEFORE the orders were issued, `main.pjs` is still 396 bytes with the unused import,
`index.html` still carries no `OPERATOR` and no queue wording. Only `mythos-theme.css` is under a changed
key - which is the proof the read is live rather than a stale cache, because the changed key is the one
thing that changed. So the verdict delivered with the order is precise: the skin is SAVED; the canon and
the room's life are a SESSION CLAIM, NOT SHIPPED. And the order's first item is therefore not a fix but a
handshake: SAVE, THEN PROVE.

**4. THE ORDER WAS RE-ISSUED, NOT SUMMARISED.** The temptation with a repeat order is to compress it -
"same as before, plus the four open items". That would have been useless to her, and dishonest about what
she can see. So the text is the full thing, verbatim, in the record, with the fresh numbers in it: the
keys, the byte counts, the four open items, the fix list, the pjs trap, the reporting shape, and the
standing line at the end. If a third room ever joins, this is the block that travels.

earlier reading existed. Both were done the other way: read it again, and write the correction beside the
