## WHAT WAS THOUGHT

**Why it is a protocol and not a style note.** The traffic between these rooms is read by a human in the
middle of carrying it, and half of what goes past is a status. A status whose verdict is in the last
paragraph costs the reader the whole report to learn one thing. Putting the conclusion first is not
politeness - it is what lets Patrikios decide *what to read next* instead of reading everything to find
out whether he needs to.

**Why "save the token" does NOT mean "write less".** Every receipt in these docs exists because a claim
without one has already cost this project a day: the CDN key that proved a save, the byte count that
proved a file, the factory fetch that settled a canon. So the rule is scoped to **order, not
completeness** - *a short report that drops a receipt is worse than a long one*. The record stays whole;
the traffic gets short. That distinction is written into both the order and the README, because "be brief"
is the kind of instruction that quietly erodes receipts if it is left loose.

## WHAT WENT WRONG

Nothing. This is the first pass in the session with no finding to report: no code changed, no receipt was
contradicted, and the only repair was applying the rule itself to the two files that describe it.
