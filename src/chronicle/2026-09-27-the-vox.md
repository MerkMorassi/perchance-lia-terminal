# THE VOX TREE

2026-09-27 - the session where a voice stopped being an attachment and became a folder, and the first
Chatterbox roundtrip was walked in the open.

## WHAT HE SAID

The session opened as a question about the shape of the whole speech lane, and it was asked as a
confirmation request rather than a build order:

> *"DO NOT CODE YET. LET'S CONFIRM FIRST: For longform narratives with custom voicevia hugging face and
> a custom voice clone sample audio (supplied):
> https://huggingface.co/spaces/ResembleAI/Chatterbox"*

Then, in his own words, the router he had in mind:

> *"Or all 3 depending on duration. Letting ELEVENLABS do all the heavy lifting is expensive,
> HuggingFace or local browser voice is free. ELEVENLABS/CHATTERBOX gives us a low-cost, cheap
> alternative if a more expressive, custom cloned voice is ever needed. the default is what we have
> now."*

And the thing that was missing, named plainly:

> *"We need an Audio-to-Text transcription service so that she can hear and see the generated text. If
> you don't have when, ROUTE to GEMINI. It has it."*

Then the folder, in the shorthand he actually writes in:

> *"Any audio she \"hears\" Hers is in the GALLERY. We need a folder for VOX by AGENT os
> AUDIO>VOX>AGENT>LIA"*

And the sample, by name, with the reason it matters:

> *"Her voice sample is a named SAMPLE you can use for CHATTERBOX that you send with her 300 Character
> Max text and SAMPLE payload if sent to HuggingFace free-tier. It sounds exactly like the browser
> SYSTEM voice (Chrome) SAMPLE: BARBELO-SYSTEM-VOICE-2.wav"*

The distinction, one message later:

> *"BARBELO-SYSTEM-VOICE-2.wav is the audio edited CLONE SAMPLE to drive CHATTERBOX for now. Music and
> VOX is not the same. VOX is vocal audio, Music is MUSIC. Gemini LYRIA can detect and analyze MUSIC
> which is useful for any music analysis and generation."*

Then: *"Sat it up and test roundtrip text and payload round trip with return CHATTERBOX audio and
confirm."* And the tab: *"create a TAB for VOX where we can place all voice samples for future
CHATTERBOX recall. I will manually place them in the right folders. Rather than \"music: they can be
labeled \"VOX\""*. And the move: *"let's move all VOX audio files to VOX. They aren't defined as any
particular lia entity yet but will be TAGGED and put in the right folder later when needed."*

## WHAT WAS UNDERSTOOD

That the lane he was describing **already existed**, and the honest answer was a confirmation rather
than a build: the live engine (ElevenLabs when a key is held, else the browser's own) speaks the
sentence, the story lane renders the chapter on Chatterbox, and the sample **IS** the voice there
(speaker conditionals, the clip uploaded as the reference). What did not exist was a *place* for a
voice to live - and a sample that cannot be found cannot be recalled.

That "Audio-to-Text" was two different things wearing one name: **hearing** (a multimodal model listens
and says what it is - what HER EARS does now) and a **verbatim transcript** (a transcription model).
The key reached both, and only then was it worth writing anything.

## WHAT WAS MEASURED, BEFORE ANYTHING WAS BUILT

**On his own Google key, a live /models call:** 50 models. Among them **`gemini-3.5-transcribe`** - a real
transcription model, which is the service he asked for, on a key he already holds - plus
`gemini-3.8-flash` / `3.7-flash` / `3.5-flash` for listening, `gemini-3.8-flash-tts` and
`gemini-2.5-flash-preview-tts` for a fourth renderer (no clone), and **`lyria-3.5`**,
**`lyria-3-pro-preview`**, `lyria-3-clip-preview` for music. The line kept straight in the reply: **Lyria
makes music; the multimodal line listens to it and says what it is.**

**The vault, audited:** 55 audio records. Exactly two are not voice -
`Beyond the Veil-MASTERED.wav` (334 s) and `Orbital Pulse-MASTERED.wav` (173 s) - and those are music.
The other **51** are voice (7-14 s each: the voice notes, the `tmp*.mp3` renders,
`malindra-audio-001.wav`, `TTS_example_audio_001.wav`).

**The sample, found unfiled:** `BARBELO-SYSTEM-VOICE-2.wav` - 13.2 MB, 74.7 s, 44.1 kHz stereo - sitting
in the vault with **no folder at all**, which meant it silently resolved to **/music**, beside the songs.
`story.state().samples` was `{}`: no agent had a voice, so a story spoke in the Space's own.
(`BARBELO-SYSTEM-VOICE.wav` turned out to be byte-identical to it.)

**THE ROUNDTRIP.** The lane's own call - `window.story.render(text, sampleRec)`, not a hand-rolled
request - with 101 characters and the clip as the reference payload:

| | with the sample payload | no sample (the control) |
|---|---|---|
| text | 101 chars | the same 101 chars |
| reference | uploaded (`refUploaded: true`) | none |
| answered in | **8.6 s** | 5.4 s |
| bytes back | **291,884** | 222,764 |
| decoded as | **6.08 s, 24 kHz mono 16-bit PCM** | 4.64 s |
| level | RMS **-16.9 dB** - real speech | - |
| road | the Space's shortcut call, `keyed` | - |

No Hugging Face key was needed. And the archive was checked rather than assumed: **1000 entries in,
1000 entries out, the same tail stamp** - the probe drove `render` directly instead of a turn, because a
test that drives real turns displaces the oldest real entries of a rolling log. Both files were kept and
handed to him to listen to side by side.

## WHAT WAS BUILT

**THE RULE, IN ONE PLACE.** `window.media.isVox(rec)` (src/js/vault.js): a record is a voice when it
says so (`kind: "vox"`) **or when it is FILED anywhere in the vox tree** - so **filing is the gesture**.
`window.media.voxFolder(rec)` answers where a voice belongs (`/audio/vox/<agent>` when the record names
one, the shelf itself when it does not, because nothing is attributed to an agent until he says so);
`defaultFolder` sends a record born `kind: "vox"` to the shelf; the shelf is seeded at boot beside
/images and /music; `LIA_MEDIA_FOLDERS.vox` is the knob.

**THE TREE.** `/audio/vox` created, `/audio/vox/barbelo` created and the two samples filed into it,
`/audio/vox/lia` created **and left empty on purpose** - a folder may exist while empty, and an empty
one is where the next voice goes.

**THE TAB.** `{ id: "vox", label: "VOX" }` in the gallery's `FILTERS`, between MUSIC and CLIPS; a `VOX`
badge in magenta (`#ff88cc`); the ♪ glyph on a voice tile where a song gets ♫; the bus button on a voice
tile too, because one transport and both are sound; and `/gallery vox` (with `voice`, `voices`,
`vocal`, `sample`, `samples`) from the terminal.

**THE SAMPLE SET.** `BARBELO-SYSTEM-VOICE-2.wav` is now **barbelo's** sample and the **`*` fallback**, so
the agent on stage (LIA HERSELF, OFF STAGE) resolves to it. Set through `window.story.setSample` - the
same door `/story sample ... as default` uses.

**AND A VOICE CAN BE RECALLED FROM THE TAB.** Every VOX tile carries **◆** (`button.gVoice`): one press
makes that clip the voice the story lane clones from - the sample of the agent on stage - through the
lane's own `window.story.setSample(rec.id)`, which is the same door `/story sample <ID>` uses, so the
receipt printed is the lane's own and there is no second way of setting a voice. A song never carries it:
the Space would clone from a song just as happily, which is the mistake the tree exists to prevent.

**THE MOVES.** 51 records filed into `/audio/vox`; the two MASTERED tracks left in `/music`; nothing
tagged, because attribution is his to do later, one agent at a time.

## WHAT WAS ALMOST THE BUG

**PROPER FOLDERS would have emptied the tree.** The folders job's one line was
`bucketPath(r) || defaultFolder(r)`, and `defaultFolder` sends every audio record to **/music**. So the
first time `/tools folders run` was pressed, all 53 voices would have been filed back among the songs -
and it would have been *correct* by its own rule, with nothing on screen to say anything had gone
wrong. That is the argument for putting the definition of "a voice" in **one place that everything
asks**: the gallery now delegates to `media.isVox` too, so the tab and the job cannot disagree. The
line is now `bucketPath(r) || (isVox(r) ? voxFolder(r) : defaultFolder(r))`, and a scan of the job in
the live preview returns **0 moves**.

## A VOICE CAN BE TAGGED, AND IT CARRIES ITS PROMPT

His next message: *"We need the ability to add tags like images. \"#LIA\", \"#ROLE\", etc. from a list of
\"name\", \"role\", \"mood\", etc."* - and then, on the way in: *"The prompt should be attached to the sample
in the event we want to upgrade the quality or accent, etc."*

**A voice could not be tagged at all.** `paintLightboxTags` hid the TAGS row for anything with an audio
mime, so a voice had neither a model reading it (as a picture has) nor a word filed on it: unfindable by
anything but its filename. The row is shown now - for every record that can carry tags, only a clip left
out - and a sound record gets two more rows on the same stage.

**THE VOICE ROW is the list he asked for, read live.** `LIA_VOX_TAGS` (src/js/config.js) names the
categories and where their values come from, and `voxTagGroups()` reads them at paint time, so a new
agent or a new mood is a chip with no code touched. NAME comes from the agent roster, MOOD from the mood
list, KIND and ENGINE are literals. One press files the word. A category with nothing to offer is not
drawn. **And ROLE is not a second group** - in this terminal a role IS an agent (the roster IS the
RoleLayer list), so a second row of the same words would be the same chips twice; if ROLE ever means
something else, it is one line in the config.

The measured chips, on his own machine: `#barbelo #meta-maternal · #tender #playful #hungry #feral #cold
#still #grieving #mythic #slutty #horny #bitchy #bossy #bratty #savage #sitient · #sample #take #test
#audition · #chatterbox #elevenlabs #browser` - the shipped moods **and the ones he wrote himself**
(#sitient among them), which is what reading the list live is for.

**THE VOICE PROMPT is his own catch, and it is the right one.** A picture carries the words that made it,
so it can be made again; a voice must carry the same or a better one can never be made. The prompt is
kept on the record (`rec.prompt`, written with `media.setField`), edited in a row on the full stage, and
printed under the sample in `/story sample`'s table - where it matters most, because choosing a voice is
exactly when he needs to remember what it was made of. **It is deliberately NOT sent to the Space**:
Chatterbox clones from the clip and no sentence steers it, so this is not conditioning - it is the
recipe for **remaking** the voice, for the upgrade he named (quality, accent) rather than for the render
in front of him.

## STILL OPEN

1. **A take is not kept.** A story part is rendered, played and forgotten, so the tree holds samples
   and only samples. Keeping them needs his answer: every part by default, or only what he marks? A
   chapter is tens of megabytes at 48 kHz stereo.
2. **A newly rendered voice still defaults to /music** unless it is filed into the tree or born
   `kind: "vox"` - the code side of the same decision.
3. **Transcription is not routed to `gemini-3.5-transcribe`.** The key reaches it; one door for the
   microphone, attachments and everything else, verbatim rather than described, is not written.
4. **Lyria** is reachable and nothing uses it - music analysis and generation are his to call for.
5. **The 74.7 s reference is long** for a clone (the lane's own guidance is 10-30 s). It worked; a
   trimmed clip would condition faster.
