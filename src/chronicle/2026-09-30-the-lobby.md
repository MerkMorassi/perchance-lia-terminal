## WHAT WAS THOUGHT

two root files; the served page's own `#preloaded-generator-data` (read through `downloadGenerator`,

done: the Factory (lorepack ingest/chunk/embed) is stubbed to the transcript, and Voice (Gemini Live,
ElevenLabs) is stubbed key inputs.

**2. THE FIFTEEN ARE NOT THE SIXTEEN, AND THE DIFFERENCE IS THE WHOLE POINT.** Its roster is
BARBELO, SOPHIA, DOMANTHEIA, ARCHIVAX, NOESIS, MERKOS and the nine Muses - the same pantheon this
terminal's `src/mythos/agents.json` is minted for - but ordered differently (its Barbelo is first, ours

**3. THE CORRECTION WAS THE REAL INSTRUCTION.** *"Here, she is not the Orchestrator of the Chat."* Two
things in the other room must never cross into this one: the phrase "orchestrator" in her role text, and
the precedence that always puts her first in a broadcast. Here she is the ACTOR wearing a layer, and the
locus the layer is minted for is order 13, not a chair. The chair of any room is Patrikios's - order 14
MERKOS is this roster's own word for him, and order 15 OPERATOR holds the stage and leaves no line in
the record. So the rule is written as a rule: **in any lobby she takes a seat like everyone else.** It is
also why the obvious cheap road - lift the room's `agents.json` as a roster - was refused: it would drag
another stage's role text into the manifest that the packs are minted against.

**4. "AS A MODULE" WAS READ AS DATA-ONLY, NOT CODE-SHARED.** No import between the two generators, no
shared `kv` folder, no file of the other page loaded here. The wire is a DOCUMENT shape - one record per
ALIA: a schema id, `id`, `handle`, `order`, `role`, and where it came from - with fixed conventions this
house already has (handles UPPERCASE, ids lowercase, order 0-15, OPERATOR 15, and
`src/mythos/agents.json` as the naming authority on this side). One direction, read-only, and a room
message that ever reaches this terminal arrives as a DOCUMENT - provenance written the ordinary way, so
its origin survives - and is never her memory. That last line is the one that keeps the two projects
from eating each other: **the room carries the room; each ALIA carries her own memory and her own role.**

**5. THE UNIX ETHOS, AS HE MEANT IT HERE.** Small things, one job each, plain text between them, pipes,
compose: the room is one filter, a terminal is another, and neither needs to know how the other is
built. Which is exactly what §1co's advice about outside surfaces already said, and why the target was
written down as a LOBBY (fifteen ALIAs in one room, channels, presence) and not as fifteen personas in
one big room. The Symposium is the inverse model of a lobby, and both are worth having.

**6. THE POINTERS WERE WRITTEN FOR THE OTHER AGENT, AND THE BUGS WERE PART OF THEM.** The room was read
closely enough to hand its agent something useful rather than a summary: the mislabelled thinking
placeholder (`askAgent` passes its own `findTarget` in, so a box can read `HANDLE → TARGET` before it has
said anything), the dead custom paths (`CUSTOMS` and custom focuses are read but nothing writes them),
the streaming that was set up and then not used (`onChunk` clears the placeholder but no text ever
streams), the unused `superFetch` import, the duplicated focus source (`roomFocus` in `main.pjs` versus
the hard-coded `FOCUS_DEFAULTS`), the fourteen-message transcript that every one of fifteen agents is
handed in full on every turn, and the `inner.innerHTML` roster cards that interpolate `bio` / `title` /
`system_instruction` (fine while he edits that file, an injection hole the moment a custom agent or an
imported roster enters). All of it is in §1cq, addressed to the other agent, in its own section.

## THE STRUCTURE

And then the constraint that binds every later build of this: *"I want to keep it light and easy to skin or
monorepo or proper UNIX ETHIC and STRUCTURE later and eventually pipe with an Orchestrated SOMA bridge
run by the LIAS."* SOMA turned out to be his own word and already written down - a copy of his
`mythos-rag-agent-voice` repo was in this session's `scratch/`, and its `services/soma.ts` says what it
is: *"Service Oriented Multi-Agent Orchestrator ... a Hypervisor for the agent cluster. It does not
generate content itself, but manages the routing, permission checks, and state of the cluster."* Around
it: a VirtualFs whose agents are FILES with owners and UNIX modes, a ShellService with a real prompt and
`ls`/`cat`/`grep`/`chmod`/`su`/`sudo`, the `MultiAgentMessage` envelope, `SomaActionType`
(`ROUTE_REQUEST`, `DELEGATE_TASK`, `COLLABORATE`...), and NumMarkX sigils - the identity this terminal's
LOREPACK already uses. So "proper UNIX structure later" is a port of types he has already written, and
the session's job was to state the seams that make the port a move rather than a rewrite: one core with
skins as tokens; one roster schema shared as DATA (id / handle / order / accessLevel 777-755-644 /
permissions); the room as a NAMESPACE (a channel is a directory, a message an append-only line in the
envelope the kernel will want: `{id, from, text, at, to[]}`); one job per tool with text in and text out;
and one kernel seam - a single `route(envelope)` that today just delivers to the room. The endgame is
agency, not plumbing: an Orchestrated SOMA bridge RUN BY THE LIAS means a node initiates, the kernel
checks the permission, provenance survives, and the human keeps admin - the same data-only rule as the
orders, with the agency moved to the nodes. No code: a constraint recorded, binding every later build of
THE LOBBY, and the light room must run with none of that machinery.
