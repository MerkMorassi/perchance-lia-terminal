# THE WORDS ON A SOUND

2026-09-27 - the session where a voice stopped being something she could only *describe*, and started
being something she can **read**: what it says, word for word, and what it means.

## WHAT HE SAID

One message, and it named the feature, the road, the test he had in mind, and the wall he already
expected:

> *"Next, let's give her some GEMINI superpowers: transcription and knowledge of what the "VOX" sample
> actually said from an audio sample. I might send her a clip in the chat and ask. She might also have
> "sentiment analysis" (GEMINI), etc. Anything else deemed important and possible with our
> PERHANCE/GEMINI setup? GEMINI may reject some text for SAFETY or POLICY guidlenes."*

Four things are in those three sentences, and they are four different requirements: a **transcript**
(the words, verbatim), **knowledge of the sample** (the vault's own voice, read), **a clip in the chat**
(the attachment road), and **sentiment** - plus an open question about what else the key can do, and a
warning that the service says no sometimes and needs to be handled honestly.

## WHAT WAS BUILT

`src/js/hear.js` - `window.hear` - **one door** with four passes, and a record that keeps what it finds.

- **`words(source, opts)`** - the verbatim transcript. The prompt asks for the words as they are said
  and nothing else, unclear words in square brackets, and the sentinel **`NO SPEECH`** when there is
  nothing spoken in it, so a piece of music gets an honest *there are no words in this* instead of an
  invented line. Written on the record as `transcript`.
- **`reading(source, opts)`** - one call, one JSON object, for the record: `speech`, `transcript`,
  `language`, `speakers`, `music`, `emotion`, `tone`, `sentiment`, `intensity`, `summary`, `phrases`,
  `title`. Written as `reading`, and where the same call also returned the words and the record had
  none, the transcript is written too - one call, both facts, nothing sent twice.
- **`ask(source, question, opts)`** - any question about a clip. Writes **nothing**: a question is a
  conversation, not a fact about the file.
- **`checkSample(source, opts)`** - the clone-reference judgement the story lane had been missing:
  usable or not, how many voices, clean or not, seconds of speech, what is wrong with it, what to
  change, and one sentence describing the voice for its **VOICE PROMPT** - suggested, never written.
- **`powers()`** - what this key can actually do, read live from its own `/models` list, with what is
  wired and what is not, so "anything else possible" is answered by the key rather than by a comment.
- **`paintStage()`** - the **WORDS** and **READING** rows on a voice's full stage, with COPY, HEAR IT
  AGAIN, READ IT AGAIN, CLEAR, and IS IT A GOOD SAMPLE?.

Wired into the four places that matter: **the vault** (the stage rows), **the chat** (`attach.js` asks
this lane FIRST for a clip handed over, before ElevenLabs/Whisper, and the words are written on the
clip's own vault record), **the commands** (`/hear words|reading|ask|sample|clear|powers`, which are
verbs of the existing `/hear` delegated to the lane from `audition.js`, so there is one verb list and
not two), and **her instruction** (the bus block in `audition.js` carries the transcript and the
reading on every turn the piece is on the bus, whichever way her ears went).

## WHAT WAS THOUGHT

**Three questions, not one.** Her ears already existed: `audition.js` reduces a sound here and has
Gemini describe it, writing `heard` on the record - *what is in it*. The request was for a different
fact, and it is worth keeping them apart in the record and in her instruction, because they answer
different questions and can disagree: a voice can *sound* tender and *say* something cruel. So
`heard` (her reading of the sound), `transcript` (the words) and `reading` (the meaning) are three
fields, and the stage shows the last two beside the tags.

**One door, and the reduction in front of it.** Every path that wants words - the vault, the chat, a
command - comes through `window.hear`, so there is no second call site to keep in step. And the sound
is reduced HERE first (mono 16 kHz, at most `LIA_HEAR_SECONDS` = 180 s): the 13 MB master leaves as
2.4 MB of speech, only those seconds go anywhere, and the file is never uploaded, re-encoded or moved.
This is the same primitive her ears use, and the same promise.

**A refusal is an answer, not an obstacle** - the requirement he named in the same breath as the
feature. In code: detected from the service's own fields (`promptFeedback.blockReason`,
`finishReason`) or, when the fields say `STOP`, from a **short answer that opens like a refusal**
(deliberately narrow, so a real transcript cannot be mistaken for one); reported in its own words with
the model that refused; **the pass stops there** - offering the same words to the next model on the
list would be shopping for permission; **nothing is written on the record**; nothing is rewritten,
shortened or softened to get past a filter; no second provider is quietly asked to take a second look.
The receipt says the quiet part out loud: *"A REFUSAL IS THE SERVICE'S ANSWER, NOT A VERDICT ON THE
CLIP - AND NOT SOMETHING THIS TERMINAL WILL QUIETLY WORK AROUND."*

One distinction was kept straight on purpose. A clip handed over in the **chat** rides a ladder of
speech-to-text services he holds keys for (`attach.js`: Gemini first, then ElevenLabs Scribe, then
Whisper on HuggingFace), and there the **same bytes** go to the next service on the list. That is not
laundering - nothing is rewritten for anybody - and it is never silent: the refusal travels back with
the words and the receipt says which road would not read it and which one did.

A **503/429 is different weather** and is treated differently: it is not a judgement on the file, so
the pass carries on down the list - and after two in a row it says so, because each one costs the
better part of a minute and 40 s of silence looks like a hang.

**The order is measured, not taste.** The list is asked of the key itself and scored (a full `flash`
beats a `pro`, a version beats an alias, `-lite` last), and which model answered is only remembered
once a pass has actually come back with something. But the measured lesson of this session is that
*quality order* and *what will answer* are not the same list, so the `-latest` aliases are pulled up to
just behind the strongest versioned model.

## WHAT WENT WRONG

1. **`gemini-3.5-transcribe` answers with nothing.** The dedicated transcriber - on the key,
   `generateContent` supported, the obvious first choice - came back `200 / finishReason: STOP / no
   text`, twice. An empty answer is now treated as a **dead end, not an answer**: it is offered the
   sound first (two seconds) and stepped over, which also means a fixed model is picked up free later.
2. **The better-ranked model was the one that 503'd.** `gemini-3.8-flash`, `3.7-flash`, `3.6-flash`
   and `3.5-flash` all came back `503 high demand` on the same audio, one after another at ~40 s each,
   while `gemini-flash-latest` and `gemini-flash-lite-latest` answered it happily - an alias serves
   whatever is current and evidently sits on different capacity. Hence the alias bump, the 10-minute
   set-aside for a model that failed (kept in kv, so a reload does not re-hammer it), and the
   two-in-a-row warning.
3. **A refusal can arrive as a sentence.** Gemini sometimes answers `STOP` with "I'm sorry, I can't
   help with that" instead of the shape it was asked for. Written as a transcript, that would have
   been a lie on the record, so a narrow opener test catches it and reports it as a refusal.
4. **Writing the transcript on the record needed an id that was being thrown away.** `attach.js`
   called `media.add(...)` and ignored its return value; it now keeps it, so the clip in the vault
   carries its own words and its full stage shows them.

## STILL OPEN

- **Lyria (`lyria-3.5`, `lyria-3-pro-preview`) is reachable and nothing uses it.** `/hear powers` names
  it as NOT WIRED. Music *generation* is a real lane here - compose, not only listen.
- `gemini-3.x-flash-tts` (speech aloud, no cloning - the story lane clones from a clip through
  Chatterbox, which is a different road, not a better one) and `gemini-3.x-flash-image` /
  `nano-banana-pro-preview` (picture making and editing) are reachable and unused.
- **`[[HEAR: ...]]`** - she cannot ask for the words of a piece herself. `[[READ: ...]]` and
  `[[FILE: ...]]` are hers, so this is the obvious next line.
- The **sentiment taxonomy is four words**: on his sample the model chose `neutral` with emotion
  `aroused`. A wider register may read better, and that is a prompt change, not a code change.
- The **74.7 s reference** is judged usable but not clean (mild noise, slight reverb); a trimmed,
  deader-room sample would condition the clone faster, which is the lane's own advice, unasked for.
