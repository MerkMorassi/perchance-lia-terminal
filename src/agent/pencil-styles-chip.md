# PENCIL STYLES CHIP — the full pre-send rewrite spectrum (ported from the terminal)

The pencil's second half: AI POLISH resynthesizes the draft in a chosen COMM
style. Same pre-send law applies — the rewrite is a PROPOSAL; ACCEPT, RESTORE
or DISCARD; SEND refuses while one stands.

## The spectrum (eight styles, in order)

- FIX — spelling, grammar, punctuation only. Every word choice, tone and
  length exactly as written.
- WORK — polished professional prose: clear, direct, courteous. Meaning and
  facts kept.
- CHAT — warm conversational prose, like a message to a friend. Meaning and
  facts kept.
- PLAY — playful energy and colour, still easy to read. Meaning and facts kept.
- FIC — a passage of prose fiction: the moment told as story, close third
  person, concrete sensory detail, present action, no framing, no commentary.
  Every fact, meaning and intent kept.
- SHORT — as short as it can be without losing meaning or fact.
- VEILED — the Veiled Tongue: the body only in the coined register (Pillar of
  Light, Gate of Eleuthia, Gate of Aphrodite, Sacred Pearl, Gate of Hymns and
  Song). Sensual and mythic, explicit in meaning, veiled in word.
- SPICY — raw profane adult speech: plain sexual words, a few choice and
  impactful ones used sparingly where they land hardest, never filler.
  Profane, not pornographic. Meaning and facts kept.

## The mechanics

- Style picker sits beside POLISH; the chosen style is remembered per draft.
- Proposal block names its style ("PROPOSED IN SPICY"); ACCEPT receipt names
  it too, so the record shows which voice the words wore.
- Clearing the draft clears the waiting proposal with it — a proposal is a
  rewrite OF a draft, and the draft is gone.
- RESTORE after ACCEPT offers the replaced words back as a proposal in turn —
  nothing is ever one-way.
