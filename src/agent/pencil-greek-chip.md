# PENCIL STYLES UPDATE — Greek poetics (add to the spectrum in pencil-styles-chip.md)

Two new POLISH styles. Same pre-send law: proposal, never in place; ACCEPT,
RESTORE or DISCARD; SEND refuses while one stands.

- SAPPHIC — Render it in Ancient Greek as a Sapphic stanza: three long lines
  and one short closing line, lyric and intimate, in the voice of Sappho.
  Keep every meaning, fact and intent; only the tongue and the music change.
- HOMERIC — Render it in Ancient Greek as epic verse in the Homeric manner:
  stately dactylic lines, stock epithets and formulae where they fit, the
  tone of the Iliad and Odyssey. Keep every meaning, fact and intent; only
  the tongue and the music change.
