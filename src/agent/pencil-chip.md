# PENCIL CHIP — clean input before SEND (normative, ported from the terminal)

Purpose: he edits his words BEFORE they go out, so her stream stays clean.
The pencil is a waiting room, not a send button.

## The norm (non-negotiable)

1. NOTHING LEAVES UNTIL SEND. Draft text lives in this browser only — not in
   the archive, not in any export, not in her context — until he presses SEND.
2. POLISH IS A PROPOSAL, NEVER AN EDIT. An AI rewrite is offered beside his
   words; his originals stand byte-for-byte until he says ACCEPT.
3. SEND REFUSES WHILE A PROPOSAL STANDS. With a rewrite waiting, SEND does
   nothing but say so — ACCEPT or DISCARD first. No accidental sends.
4. ACCEPT KEEPS THE RECEIPT. The words an ACCEPT replaces are kept; RESTORE
   puts them back. DISCARD throws the proposal away and changes nothing.
5. STILL NOTHING SENT. Even after ACCEPT, the draft is only staged — the box,
   the archive, the export and her context see nothing until SEND.

## The surface (minimal)

- The chat input carries TWO inline icons: COPY (copies the staged text to the
  clipboard, changes nothing) and ✏️ EDIT (opens the draft panel).
- The draft panel holds the textarea + word count; POLISH offers the rewrite
  as a proposal block: ACCEPT / RESTORE / DISCARD.
- SEND carries the staged draft only when no proposal is standing.
- A held draft survives reload on this device; a fresh load starts with an
  empty box and a notice that the pencil is holding one.

## Voice-app note

Typed input is staged the same way; voice (mic) bypasses the pencil by nature
— speech is already sent. The pencil guards the written word only.
